Privacy Policy

Plain version: I collect what I need to reply to you and build your site. I do not sell it, and you can have it deleted whenever you ask.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Template notice. This policy is a starting point written for a small web-design studio. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it, especially if you take payments online or contact people outside the United States.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to mbonyx.netlify.app and any site operated by MBOnyx Studio ("we", "I", "us"). Contact: mbonyxstudios@gmail.com, Liberty, Missouri.

2. Information collected from you

When you submit the contact form

  • Your name and email address
  • The package you selected, and how you heard about us
  • Anything you type into the project details field

This is used solely to reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote and, if you hire us, deliver the project. Form submissions are processed by Netlify Forms, our hosting provider.

When you simply visit

Our web host records standard server logs — IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps — for security and troubleshooting. If analytics are enabled, they are configured to measure page views in aggregate, not to identify you individually.

3. Business contact information we gather

We conduct business-to-business outreach. As part of that we may collect publicly listed business contact information — a company name, its public website, a general business phone number and a published business email address such as info@ or contact@ — from public sources including business directories, mapping services and the company's own website.

We also record technical measurements of a company's public website, such as its load time, mobile compatibility and security certificate status.

  • We collect this only about businesses, never about private individuals.
  • We do not buy or sell contact lists.
  • We do not guess, generate or purchase personal email addresses.
  • We do not attempt to access any private or password-protected area of any website.

Opting out

Every email we send carries a working unsubscribe link and our postal address. You can also email mbonyxstudios@gmail.com with the word REMOVE and we will delete your record and add you to a permanent suppression list so you are never contacted again. Removal requests are honoured within 10 business days and usually within one.

4. How information is used

  • To reply to inquiries and provide quotes
  • To deliver, host and support projects you have hired us for
  • To send one-to-one business correspondence
  • To keep records required for accounting and tax
  • To maintain a suppression list of people who asked not to be contacted

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling of individuals.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, each bound to protect it:

  • Netlify — website hosting and form processing
  • Our email provider — sending and receiving correspondence
  • Our accountant — invoices and tax records, where legally required

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Cookies

This site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Any cookies present are strictly functional, and you can block them in your browser without breaking the site.

7. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don't become projects — up to 24 months, then deleted
  • Client project records — 7 years, for tax and warranty purposes
  • Suppression list entries — kept indefinitely, because that is the only way to honour a removal request permanently

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you

Email mbonyxstudios@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days at the latest. Residents of California, the EU, the UK and other regions with specific statutory rights may exercise them through the same address, at no charge.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to inquiry data is restricted to the studio owner. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as passwords or payment card numbers through the contact form.

10. Children

This is a business service not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will be updated. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.
