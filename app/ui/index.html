Lead Checker

Finds businesses whose websites need rebuilding. Audits only — it never emails anyone.

Finds who is there using OpenStreetMap, then goes and looks for each one’s website — free, no account, nothing to sign up for. Pick an area and it works through every town in it, moving to the next when one runs out and saving as it goes — so stopping never loses anything, and starting again adds to what you have. Press Stop when you have enough, then check them.

One per line. Format: name, website, phone, rating, review count — only the website is required. The rating and review count come off the Google listing and tell the checker who can afford you.

US law requires a real postal address in a commercial email — a PO box counts, and one costs a few dollars a month at the post office. Until you have one, the drafts below are for practice, not for sending.

What to look for 
For each Tier A and B business, honestly:

  1. Would you actually pay someone to rebuild this site?
  2. Open one on your real phone — is the finding true?
  3. Anything scoring high that is actually fine?      (false positive)
  4. Anything scoring low that is obviously terrible?  (a miss — worse)

Tier A  70+   contact now
Tier B  50-69 worth contacting
Tier C  25-49 hold, re-check in six months
Tier D  under 25 — the site is fine, drop it
