Before we begin

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the questions clients usually ask before starting a project.

Still unsure about something? Include it in your inquiry and I’ll answer it directly.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is written by hand, which is why they load in under two seconds instead of the eight-plus that page builders average. It also means nothing breaks when a plugin updates itself at 3am.

Are there monthly fees?

Not unless you want them. You own your site outright the day it launches — the code, the domain, all of it. The $99/month Care Plan is there if you would rather I handle hosting, backups and edits, and you can cancel it any time without losing your site.

How long does it take?

Starter goes live in about a week. Business is typically two weeks, and Signature two to three depending on booking or store setup. The clock starts once I have your content — that is usually the part that decides the timeline.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, any photos you want used, and a rough idea of what you want each page to say. If you do not have copy written, I will draft it and you edit — most people find that far easier than starting from a blank page.

What if I already have a website?

Then I rebuild it without losing what already works. Your domain stays yours, your Google ranking carries over through proper redirects, and your existing site stays live right up until the new one is ready to switch on.

How does the referral payment work?

If someone names you as their referrer, you get 15% of what they pay, sent within 14 days of their final payment clearing. You earn on businesses you refer directly — one level, no chains or downlines. There is no cap on how many you can refer, and you do not have to be a client yourself.

Can I update it myself afterwards?

Yes. On Business and Signature I hand over a short walkthrough video showing exactly how to change your hours, prices and photos. If you would rather not, that is what the Care Plan is for.
