Our Story

A corner shop that refused to scale.

We opened in 2014 with six tables and one espresso machine. People kept telling us to open a second location. We kept saying no.

What we did instead was get better at the twelve things we actually make — and learn every regular's order by heart.

— Tess & Marco, owners
